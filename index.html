How do I request an insurance quote?

Choose a plan and select Get a quote. Add your details and open WhatsApp to review and send your enquiry to Sigma.

Can I enquire about cover for my family and business together?

Yes. Choose a primary plan and list the other plans in your message. This helps the team direct each part of your enquiry.

What should I prepare for a renewal?

Have your current policy schedule, renewal date and any changes in your circumstances ready. Your broker can then discuss what to review.

What is an excess?

An excess is the portion of a covered claim that you bear under the policy terms. Ask where it applies and whether different excesses apply to different claims.
