Help centre

Your questions,
answered.

A starting point for choosing cover, requesting a quote and preparing for renewal.

How do I request an insurance quote?

Choose a plan and select Get a quote. Add your details and open WhatsApp to review and send your enquiry to Sigma.

Can I enquire about cover for my family and business together?

Yes. Choose a primary plan and list the other plans in your message. This helps the team direct each part of your enquiry.

What should I prepare for a renewal?

Have your current policy schedule, renewal date and any changes in your circumstances ready. Your broker can then discuss what to review.

What is an excess?

An excess is the portion of a covered claim that you bear under the policy terms. Ask where it applies and whether different excesses apply to different claims.

How do I compare medical plans?

Start with your preferred hospitals, clinics and budget. Compare provider networks, co-payments, exclusions and benefit limits in the insurer’s documents.

Does requesting a quote start my insurance?

A quote is an enquiry. Check the insurer’s written confirmation, policy schedule and effective date before relying on cover.

Who should I contact about a claim?

Follow the notification instructions in your policy and keep relevant records. You can also contact Sigma for help locating the insurer’s claims contact details.

Where is the Sigma office?

You can find Sigma Insurance Brokers LLC at Office 610, Mai Tower, Al Qusais, Dubai. Contact the team to arrange a visit.
